💡 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan equipment?

Use the built-in barcode or QR scanner from the main dashboard to quickly scan items and update locations.

How do I add pre-existing equipment?

Navigate to the “Add Equipment” screen, scan an existing barcode, or manually enter details and assign a category.

How does location tracking work?

The app stores the last known geolocation when you scan equipment at a work location. This helps identify where items were last checked.

How do alerts work?

You can receive alerts when equipment is left behind or not checked out/in properly. Customize alerts in the Settings menu.
